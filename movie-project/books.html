Gunahon Ka Devta

Dharamvir Bharati

Ek aisi prem kahani jahan mohabbat, samaj aur zimmedari ek doosre se takraate hain. Ek kahani jo sirf padhi nahi jaati, mehsoos ki jaati hai.

"Kuchh rishte naam ke mohtaaj nahi hote, bas dil mein apni jagah bana lete hain."

Kuchh kahaniyan khatam nahi hoti.
